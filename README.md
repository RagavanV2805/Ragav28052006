## 📱 CareerOS (iOS)

A production-quality, offline-first **career roadmap & skill-development app**
for students and early-career developers — built natively with **Swift,
SwiftUI, SwiftData, MVVM, Swift Charts, WidgetKit and UserNotifications**.

Choose one of 14 target roles, rate your current skills, and get a
personalised, prerequisite-aware learning roadmap with a "what should I learn
next" engine, role matching, project recommendations, analytics and Home
Screen widgets.
